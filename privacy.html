PRIVACY

YOUR DATA,
PLAINLY.

What we collect when you fill in a form, why, and how to have it deleted.

Who is responsible

Licter SAS, 173 rue de Courcelles, 75017 Paris, France. Contact: contact@licter.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your work email or phone number, and, depending on the form, your name, company, sector, brand, the question you want to answer, or your answers to the diagnostic.

Our host also keeps technical logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for a short time, for security.

If you accept the audience measurement cookies, Google Analytics also records how the site is used: pages viewed, clicks on our buttons, forms sent and the score of the diagnostic, without your name or your email. See Cookies below.

Why

To reply to you, send you what you asked for (a guide, an example, a magazine, a meeting invitation, a callback) and, if you subscribed, our newsletter. Legal basis: your consent, and our legitimate interest in answering a request you made.

Who sees it

The Licter team only, and the providers that run the site and our email and client relationship tools, who process it on our behalf. We do not sell or rent your data.

Some of these providers, starting with our host, are based in the United States; transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long

Three years after our last exchange, as the CNIL recommends for prospects, then it is deleted. The newsletter stops as soon as you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, limit or object to its use, ask for a copy, or withdraw your consent at any time by writing to contact@licter.com. Each email we send has an unsubscribe link. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Cookies

This site measures its audience with Google Analytics 4 (pages viewed, time spent, use of our forms and buttons), only if you accept it in the banner shown on your first visit. Google Analytics then sets the cookies _ga and _ga_FMNYXB14XW, kept 13 months at most.

If you decline, nothing from Google is loaded and no cookie is set. Your choice is kept six months; you can change it at any time with the "Cookies" link at the bottom of every page.

The data is processed by Google Ireland Limited. Some of it may be transferred to the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. This site sets no advertising cookie.

Your theme and language, your cookie choice and the windows you have closed are remembered in your browser only and never sent to us.

Interview videos play from youtube-nocookie.com, and only once you start them.

Stay ahead of your market.

One email when we publish something worth your time: a method, a market read, or a correction. No sequence, no drip campaign.

We use your email only to reply to you. Privacy policy.
