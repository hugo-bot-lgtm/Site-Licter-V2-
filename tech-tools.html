Five questions to ask before buying a social listening licence.

We run these platforms every day, for brands of every size. Before you sign, these are the questions we would ask.

  1. What decision should the data inform?

    A platform is chosen by the question, not by the demo. A crisis, a campaign, the competition or an audience: each calls for other sources and other settings.

  2. Which sources are really covered?

    TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, forums, reviews, press: access differs from one tool to another, depending on its agreements with each network. Ask for the list, and for a test on your own brand.

  3. Who will write and maintain the queries?

    A badly written Boolean query brings back noise or misses what matters. Plan the time, or the person, to keep it up to date.

  4. Who will read the results, and when?

    A dashboard decides nothing. Without someone reading it regularly and turning it into decisions, a licence is little used.

  5. What does the whole cost, not just the licence?

    Add the users, the history, the AI modules and the time your team will spend. That total is what to compare with a service.

Frequently asked questions about social listening tools.

What is a social listening tool?

A platform that collects public posts from social networks, forums, review sites and the press, filters them with keyword queries and turns them into indicators: volume, tone, topics, reach. It shows what is said; reading it and deciding what to do with it remains human work.

Which social listening tool should you choose?

The one that answers your question. A crisis calls for real-time alerts, an audience study for a behavioural panel, a campaign for long history and image recognition. We run 19 tools and pick them by the question, never the other way round.

Social listening tool or social listening software: is there a difference?

No, the words mean the same thing: a platform that collects public posts, news and reviews and turns them into dashboards. What makes the difference is who writes the queries and reads the results. Software alone gives you data; an analyst gives you a decision.

Buy a licence or work with a social listening agency?

A licence makes sense if someone in your team can write the queries and read the results every week. Otherwise, an agency runs the tool for you and delivers the reading: you pay for the answer, not for the access.

Does Licter sell social listening tools?

No. Licter is an independent consultancy: we publish no tool, we run the platforms on the market for our clients and choose them by the question.

How much does a social listening tool cost?

It depends on the publisher, the number of users, the history and the modules. When we run the tool for you, the whole service is on quotation, licence included.
