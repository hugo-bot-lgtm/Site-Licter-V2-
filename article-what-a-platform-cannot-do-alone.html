MONITORING & SOCIAL LISTENING

What a platform cannot do on its own

Listening suites are very good at three things and structurally bad at two others. Knowing which is which is most of what separates a useful setup from an expensive one.

4 September 20262 min readLicter analysis team

Cet article n’est disponible qu’en anglais. Le reste du site bascule en français.

What they do well

Collection at scale, deduplication, and retrieval. A modern suite will pull more sources, more reliably, and hold more history than anything you would build. That is genuinely hard engineering and it is worth paying for.

Classification against a taxonomy you defined. Once the categories are right, the machine applies them consistently, which is something a human panel does not do across ten thousand items.

Alerting on thresholds you set. Reliable, fast, and indifferent to whether it is Sunday.

Every query encodes a hypothesis about what counts. The software cannot make that call for you.

What they cannot do

Decide what the perimeter is. Every query encodes a hypothesis about what counts. Include the competitor and you are measuring a category; exclude them and you are measuring a brand. Neither is wrong, but the software cannot make that call, and a perimeter chosen by default is a perimeter chosen by someone who was not thinking about your question.

Tell you what the result means. Sentiment moved four points. That is a fact. Whether it is a crisis, a seasonal artefact, or a competitor’s campaign spilling into your feed is an interpretation, and interpretation is where the value is.

The setup that follows

If the platform is good at collection and classification and bad at framing and interpretation, the sensible arrangement is obvious: buy the platform, staff the framing, and never confuse a licence with a capability.

The failure mode we see most often is the reverse. An organisation buys seats, hands them to a team with no time to define the perimeter, and receives a dashboard nobody opens. The tool was never the problem.

What to take away

  • Platforms are strong on collection, classification and alerting.
  • They cannot choose your perimeter or interpret a move.
  • A perimeter chosen by default was chosen by someone not thinking about your question.
  • Buy the licence, staff the framing.
